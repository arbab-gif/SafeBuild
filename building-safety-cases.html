1. Building Summary

Riverside House is an 18-storey residential building completed in 2020, comprising 128 units. The building is classified as a Higher-Risk Building under the Building Safety Act 2022.

The building is of reinforced concrete frame construction with brickwork and aluminium curtain walling. A composite aluminium (ACM) rainscreen system is installed to the upper levels.

Common areas include two passenger lifts, protected stairwells, and a communal roof terrace. The building is occupied by a diverse community of residents.

Mitigation Strategy & Control Measures
AI-Assisted Draft
Auto-saved
Version 3.1 (Draft)
Last saved 2 mins ago

Our mitigation strategy is focused on reducing the risk of fire spread, ensuring safe evacuation, and protecting residents through a structured programme of works, inspections, and communication. We are prioritising high-risk issues and taking a proportionate, phased approach.

Key measures include external wall remediation to remove combustible materials, a comprehensive fire stopping programme, and repairs to compartmentation to maintain fire integrity. Fire doors will be replaced to achieve appropriate standards, and smoke ventilation systems will be serviced to ensure effective operation.

We continue to review and test evacuation arrangements, update lift emergency procedures, and ensure residents are informed and engaged throughout works. All interventions are managed by competent contractors with robust quality assurance and evidence capture.

Effectiveness will be monitored through inspections, testing, and post-completion verification, with outcomes tracked in the register below.

Resident Engagement Strategy
AI Assisted Draft Version 1.2 (Draft) •
Auto-saved 2 min ago

Riverside House is committed to placing residents at the heart of our building safety approach. We keep residents informed, consulted and supported through clear, accessible and timely communication.

We will use a range of channels including letters, SMS, resident meetings, our resident app and home visits to ensure information reaches all households, with support for those who need it. We will consult residents on proposed safety works and service changes and respond to feedback transparently.

We maintain a resident panel and hold regular engagement sessions and surveys to understand concerns and priorities. We work with our Resident Liaison Officer to support vulnerable residents and ensure reasonable adjustments are provided.
