Q&A
Q: Do you cover emergency call-outs outside of business hours?
Asked by Sarah Johnson, ABC Housing · 1 month ago
Yes — we operate a 24/7 emergency response service. Our target response time is 90 minutes for urgent issues. Emergency call-outs are priced at our out-of-hours rate starting from £195/visit.
Q: What areas do you cover and do you charge travel?
Asked by James Patel, Orbit Group · 2 months ago
We currently cover Kent, Surrey, and surrounding London boroughs. No travel charge is applied for jobs within our primary coverage area (up to 50 miles from our London base). National coverage is available for framework contracts.
Q: Are all operatives DBS checked and insured?
Asked by Emma Wilson, PGR Housing · 3 months ago
Yes — all operatives hold enhanced DBS checks (renewed 2024). We carry £10M public liability and £10M employers' liability insurance. All certifications are uploaded to SafeBuild and available to view in the Certifications tab.
